Privacy Policy

Your data stays on your device.

Last updated: May 2025

Just Reps is built on a simple principle: your workout data is yours. We don't collect it, we don't store it on servers, and we don't share it with anyone.

What we collect

Nothing.

Just Reps does not collect any personal data. There is no account creation, no sign-in, and no data transmitted to external servers.

What stays on your device

All data created in Just Reps is stored locally on your iPhone using Apple's SwiftData framework. This includes:

  • Your rep logs and workout history
  • Your exercise goals and preferences
  • Your streak data
  • Your notification settings

This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly initiate a transfer (such as an iTunes backup or, in a future version, iCloud sync — which you would opt into).

Analytics

Just Reps does not use any third-party analytics SDKs, crash reporting services, or advertising networks. We do not track how you use the app.

Apple may collect anonymised, aggregated analytics through the App Store if you have opted into sharing usage data with Apple developers. This is controlled by your iPhone's Privacy settings and is entirely separate from Just Reps.

Notifications

Daily reminders are opt-in. If you enable notifications, they are scheduled locally on your device using iOS's local notification system. No notification data is sent to any server.

You can disable notifications at any time in Settings → Notifications → Just Reps, or within the app's Settings screen.

Purchases

In-app purchases (the one-time Premium unlock) are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. Just Reps does not handle payment information, does not store purchase records, and does not have access to your payment details.

Children's privacy

Just Reps does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children. Because no data is collected, the app is appropriate for all ages.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Significant changes will also be noted in the App Store release notes.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Reach us at privacy@justreps.app.

Just Reps is an indie iOS app. We have no VC funding, no growth targets, and no incentive to monetise your data.
