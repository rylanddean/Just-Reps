Streaks

How does the streak work?

Your rep streak goes up by one for each day you log at least one rep of any exercise. Log every day, keep it alive. Miss a day, it resets to zero.

What's the goal streak?

The goal streak tracks how many consecutive days you've met every active exercise goal. It's stricter than the rep streak — you need to hit all your targets, not just log something.

Does the streak reset at midnight?

No. Just Reps uses a 3 AM grace window. If you finish a late workout before 3 AM, it still counts for the previous day. Night owls welcome.

I logged reps but my streak reset. What happened?

This usually means there's a gap day between your last entry and today. Check your history to confirm. If everything looks correct and the streak is still wrong, contact us.

Can I freeze my streak?

Not yet. Streak freeze is on the roadmap. For now, the streak is what it is — honest and unpadded.

Exercises & Goals

How do I add or remove exercises?

Go to Settings → Exercises. Toggle any built-in exercise on or off. You can also add custom exercises by name.

How do I change my daily goal?

Go to Settings → Goals. Each active exercise has its own goal you can adjust with the stepper.

What is the Stretching exercise?

Stretching counts sessions, not reps. Log one session to mark it done for the day. The goal increments by 1 (not 5 like other exercises) because one session a day is the target.

What are goal suggestions?

After you've logged at least four days of data, Just Reps looks at the past week — how often you met your goal and how hard it felt — and suggests a small adjustment (±15%). You can apply it with one tap or keep your current goal. Stretching is excluded from suggestions.

Apple Health

Why does Just Reps request Health access?

Just Reps reads your Apple Health exercise minutes to calculate your training load — a rolling 7-day picture of how much you've been moving. This is used to make goal suggestions more accurate. The app never writes to Apple Health.

How do I grant or revoke Health access?

Go to Settings app → Health → Data Access & Devices → Just Reps. You can toggle read access there at any time.

What is training load?

Training load shows today's exercise minutes, your weekly total, and whether your load is trending up, down, or staying flat compared to recent weeks. It's shown on the home screen and on Apple Watch. If the trend is rising and your effort ratings are high, Just Reps may suggest easing back on a goal.

Apple Watch

Does Just Reps have an Apple Watch app?

Yes. The Watch app lets you log reps, check your streaks, view training load, see goal suggestions, and track milestones — all from your wrist.

How does the Watch app sync with iPhone?

The Watch app reads from the same shared database as the iPhone app. Workout entries you log on either device are visible on both. Goals and preferences sync automatically through a shared container.

The Watch app isn't showing my latest data. What do I do?

Make sure both your iPhone and Apple Watch are on the same Wi-Fi or within Bluetooth range. Open the iPhone app once to trigger a sync. If training load isn't current on the Watch, open the iPhone app and let it fetch from Apple Health, then check the Watch again.

What milestones does the app track?

Just Reps marks streak milestones at 3, 7, 14, 30, 60, and 100 days — for both rep streak and goal streak. You'll see progress toward the next milestone on the Watch milestones screen.

Backup & Restore

How do I back up my data?

Go to Settings → Backup & Restore → Export Backup. This creates a JSON file you can save to Files, iCloud Drive, or anywhere you like.

How do I restore from a backup?

Go to Settings → Backup & Restore → Import Backup. Select your backup file. Your full history — including effort ratings — will be restored.

Does a restore overwrite my current data?

Yes. Importing a backup replaces your current workout history. Export a fresh backup first if you want to preserve it.

Does my backup include everything?

Yes. The backup includes all workout entries, exercise types, rep counts, timestamps, and effort ratings (RPE). Goals and preferences are stored separately in Settings and are not included in the backup file.

Premium

What does Premium unlock?

The core app — logging, streaks, history, goal suggestions, training load, Apple Watch — is free. Premium is a one-time purchase that unlocks additional themes and advanced analytics.

How do I restore a purchase I already made?

Go to Settings → Premium → Restore Purchase. This works across devices signed into the same Apple ID.
