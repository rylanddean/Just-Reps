FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE — MAY 2026

Just Reps 1.3 — friends, streak-at-risk warnings, and a HealthKit fix

May 2026 — Just Reps, the minimalist iOS fitness streak app, releases version 1.3. The update adds a Friends tab powered by Game Center, a second at-risk notification for users with a streak to protect, and a correction to how rep sessions are credited to Apple Health.

The update adds the first social surface in Just Reps — deliberately narrow. Friends is a leaderboard of streaks, nothing more.

What's new

Friends. A new tab shows where you and your Game Center friends rank by current rep streak. No posts, no likes, no activity feed. Just one number next to each name: how many days in a row they've shown up.

Streak-at-risk notification. A second notification now fires at 8 PM when a user has an active streak but hasn't logged today. It auto-cancels the moment reps are logged. Tapping it opens a sheet showing which exercises still need reps and offers a streak freeze. The message is unchanged: "Your streak is at risk. Still time."

Widget and Watch warnings. The small widget, medium widget, lock screen inline widget, and all Watch complications now swap to a ⚠ warning display after 8 PM when the streak is at risk — consistent with the threshold used elsewhere in the app.

Watch freeze bars. Freeze days in the Watch activity bar chart now render as flat blue bars, visually distinct from logged days and zero days.

HealthKit ring credit fix. Previous versions calculated Apple Health exercise credit using a reps-per-minute formula, which over- or under-credited depending on exercise pace. Version 1.3 replaces this with a flat 2-minute credit per rep-count log. Timer-based exercises — plank and stretching — keep their accurate durations. The reps-per-minute setting has been removed.

What didn't change

One-tap logging is unchanged. The Friends tab is additive — it doesn't appear in the log flow and doesn't interrupt anything. The at-risk notification only fires if the user already has a streak to protect, and cancels itself the moment they log.

Pricing and availability

Just Reps 1.3 is a free update for all existing users. The app remains free to download with no subscription and no paywall.

FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE — MAY 2026

Just Reps 1.2 — home screen widgets, Siri shortcuts, and reminders that learn when you train

May 2026 — Just Reps, the minimalist iOS fitness streak app, is releasing version 1.2. The update adds home screen and lock screen widgets, Siri data queries, adaptive reminder timing, and a round of polish to the core logging experience.

The update stays true to the app's premise: remove every barrier between intention and action. None of the new features add steps to the core log flow.

What's new

Home screen widgets. Three sizes — a small streak count, a medium dual-streak panel with an 8-week heatmap, and a lock screen inline widget. The streak count shifts to red after 8 PM if no reps have been logged. Widgets update automatically on every log; no manual refresh.

Siri shortcuts. Users can ask Siri for their current streak, today's rep count, or their longest streak. The answers come from on-device data via App Intents — no account, no server.

Smart reminder timing. Just Reps now observes when a user typically logs and schedules the at-risk notification accordingly — at the median log hour plus three hours. The reminder shifts as habits shift. Users who prefer a fixed time can override it in Settings. The fallback for new users is 8 PM.

Inline goal editing. Daily goals can now be adjusted directly from the home screen. No trip to Settings required.

Streak explainer. Tapping the streak header now surfaces a plain-language explanation of how the streak is calculated — including the 3 AM grace window and how rest days and streak freezes interact.

What didn't change

One-tap logging is unchanged. The core loop — open, tap, done — takes the same number of taps it always has. The new features are ambient or opt-in; none of them surface in the main flow unless the user goes looking.

Pricing and availability

Just Reps 1.2 is a free update for all existing users. The app remains free to download with no subscription and no paywall.

FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE — MAY 2025

Just Reps launches on iOS — a fitness streak app that gets out of your way

May 2025 — Just Reps, a new iOS app focused entirely on daily rep logging and streak building, is now available on the App Store. The app was built around a single premise: removing every barrier between intention and action.

Where most fitness apps add complexity — programs, assessments, social feeds, gamification — Just Reps does the opposite. Open the app, tap a button, log your reps. That's it.

The problem it solves

Fitness apps have a well-documented consistency problem. The more features an app adds, the higher the cognitive load, and the easier it becomes to skip. Just Reps treats logging as the feature. Everything else is secondary.

"Most fitness apps are built for people who are already motivated," the developer said. "Just Reps is built for everyone else — for the days when you don't feel like it, and five reps is still five reps."

How it works

Users pick one or more exercises — pushups, squats, pull-ups, sit-ups, planks, or a custom exercise — and set a daily rep goal. Each day they log reps with large, pill-shaped tap targets. The app tracks two separate streaks: a Rep Streak for any reps logged, and a Goal Streak for days the target is met.

A GitHub-style heatmap calendar shows consistency over the past 20 weeks. A quiet notification (opt-in, never mandatory) can remind users later in the day if they haven't logged yet. After 8 PM with no reps logged, the app shows a single low-key message: "Your streak is at risk. Still time." Nothing more.

Design philosophy

Just Reps is dark-first, using a pure black background and a muted green accent that only appears when something positive happens. The app has no confetti, no fake trophies awarded for basics, no streak-shaming on missed days. The completion message when all goals are met reads: "Nice work. Now go live your life."

All data is stored locally on device. No account is required. No data leaves the phone.

Pricing

Just Reps is free to download and free to use. The full streak experience — logging, dual streaks, heatmap, history, achievements — is available to everyone, with no time limit and no paywall.

Availability

Just Reps is available now on the App Store, worldwide, for iPhone running iOS 17 or later.

About Just Reps

Just Reps is an independently developed iOS app. It is not venture-backed. It has no growth targets, no subscription revenue model, and no incentive to maximise screen time. The goal is to be the last fitness-logging app someone needs to try.

Key facts for coverage
