Features

Remove every barrier
between intention and action.

Just Reps strips fitness tracking down to what actually matters: showing up.

One-tap logging
Tap +5, +10, or +25 to log reps instantly. No navigation, no setup, no friction.
Dual streaks
Track your Rep Streak (any reps logged) and Goal Streak (daily goal met) side by side.
Heatmap calendar
A GitHub-style contribution grid that shows your consistency over the last 20 weeks at a glance.
Quiet notifications
Opt-in daily reminders. No badge counts, no guilt, no streak-shaming. Just a gentle nudge.
Workout history
A clean timeline of every session, grouped by day. No charts to maintain — just the record.
Daily goal setting
Set a rep target for each exercise. Lower it freely — easing back is smart, not failure.
Achievements
Earned quietly. Milestones unlock without fanfare — no pop-ups, no confetti, no fake coins.
Dark-first design
Built for the phone you actually use. Full dark and light mode support, no eye strain.
Fully offline
All data lives on your device. No account required, no server to trust, no sync to wait for.
