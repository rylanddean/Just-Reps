Features

Remove every barrier
between intention and action.

Just Reps strips fitness tracking down to what actually matters: showing up.

One-tap logging
Tap +5, +10, or +25 to log reps instantly. No navigation, no setup, no friction.
Dual streaks
Track your Rep Streak (any reps logged) and Goal Streak (daily goal met) side by side.
Apple Watch app
Log reps, check streaks, review goal suggestions, and track training load — all from your wrist.
Heatmap calendar
A GitHub-style contribution grid that shows your consistency over the last 20 weeks at a glance.
Training load
Weekly exercise trend from Apple Health. See whether your load is rising, steady, or easing off — before you push harder.
Milestones
Earned quietly. Streak milestones unlock without fanfare — no pop-ups, no confetti, no fake coins.
Effort tracking
Rate each set Easy, Moderate, Hard, or All Out. Logged to Apple Health and used to calibrate goal suggestions.
Intelligent goals
Looks at 7 days of data — goals met, effort ratings, and training load — to suggest when to push and when to ease back.
Six exercise types
Pushups, squats, pullups, sit-ups, plank, and stretching. Add custom exercises. Stretching counts sessions, not reps.
Quiet notifications
Opt-in daily reminders. No badge counts, no guilt, no streak-shaming. Just a gentle nudge.
Workout history
A clean timeline of every session, grouped by day. No charts to maintain — just the record.
Fully offline
All data lives on your device. No account required, no server to trust, no sync to wait for.
